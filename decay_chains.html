Radioactive Decay Chains

Visualizing the journey of a heavy element to stability.

Overview

Heavy elements like Uranium are often unstable. They undergo a series of radioactive decays, transforming into different elements, until they finally reach a stable configuration. This sequence is called a decay chain. The two most common types of decay in these chains are alpha decay (losing 2 protons and 2 neutrons) and beta decay (a neutron becomes a proton). This simulation visualizes the famous "Uranium Series," which starts at Uranium-238 and proceeds through 14 steps to end at the stable isotope Lead-206.
