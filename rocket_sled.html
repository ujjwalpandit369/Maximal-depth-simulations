Rocket Sled with Variable Mass

Demonstrating Newton's Second Law when mass isn't constant.

Overview

We often learn Newton's Second Law as F=ma, but this assumes mass (m) is constant. What happens when the mass of an object changes, like a rocket burning fuel? This simulation explores this scenario with a rocket sled. The sled has a fixed thrust, but its total mass decreases as it consumes fuel. According to the rearranged formula, a = F/m, a decreasing mass results in an increasing acceleration. This is a fundamental principle of rocketry and explains why rockets accelerate faster as they fly.
