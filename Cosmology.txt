17. The Integrated Sachs-Wolfe (ISW) Effect
Create a simulation explaining a secondary anisotropy in the CMB.
Scene: A CMB photon travels through the universe. It passes through a large supercluster and then a large void.
Guide: When a CMB photon falls into a potential well (like a galaxy cluster), it gets blueshifted. When it climbs out, it gets redshifted. In a static universe, these effects cancel perfectly. Visuals: An animation of the photon passing through a cluster that is visibly "shallowing out" over time. A readout of the photon's energy shows a net gain. This explains why there's a correlation between the CMB temperature and the large-scale structure of the nearby universe.
