The Three-Body Gravitational Problem

An interactive exploration of chaotic orbital mechanics.

Overview

This simulation demonstrates the famous **three-body problem**. While the two-body problem is perfectly solvable, the three-body problem has no general analytical solution and its behavior is often **chaotic**. Use the controls to set the initial conditions and watch the intricate, unpredictable dance they perform.

Beginner's Guide

The **three-body problem** describes the motion of three objects interacting gravitationally. Unlike the simple, predictable orbits of a two-body system (like Earth and the Sun), the three-body system is famously chaotic. A tiny change in the starting position or velocity of one body can lead to a drastically different outcome. The "Launch Twin" button demonstrates this by running a second, invisible simulation with a minuscule change, then revealing its wildly different path after a few moments.
