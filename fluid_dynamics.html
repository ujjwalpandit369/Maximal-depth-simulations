Guide to the Fluid Sandbox

This is an interactive fluid dynamics simulation based on a simplified Navier-Stokes solver. It allows you to visualize how fluids flow, mix, and interact with obstacles.

  • Add Dye: Select a color and click on the canvas to add a continuous source of dye. The dye is passive and is simply carried along by the fluid's velocity.
  • Apply Force (Stir): Click and drag on the canvas to apply forces to the fluid, creating swirls and vortices.
  • Add Obstacle: Click and drag to draw solid, immovable obstacles. Watch how the fluid flows around them. Try creating a cylinder in the middle of the inflow to see a von Kármán vortex street form behind it.
  • Viscosity: This slider controls the "thickness" of the fluid. At low viscosity, you can create complex, turbulent-like patterns. At high viscosity, the flow becomes thick and syrupy, and vortices quickly dissipate.
  • Inflow Speed: This controls a constant flow of fluid from the left side of the simulation.
