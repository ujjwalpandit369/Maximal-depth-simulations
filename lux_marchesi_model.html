The Lux-Marchesi Agent-Based Model

An econophysics model of financial market dynamics.

Overview

Why do financial markets experience periods of calm followed by sudden, violent crashes or bubbles? The Lux-Marchesi model is an influential agent-based model from econophysics that provides an explanation. It populates a market with different types of traders: fundamentalists, who believe the price will return to a rational value, and chartists, who follow recent trends. Crucially, agents can switch strategies based on which approach seems more profitable. This simulation demonstrates how this strategy-switching can create complex feedback loops, leading to emergent phenomena like volatility clustering—a key "stylized fact" of real financial markets.
