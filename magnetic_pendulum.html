Guide: The Beauty of Unpredictability

The Setup

Imagine a pendulum swinging over a table with three strong magnets. The pendulum is pulled towards the center by gravity and slowed down by friction (damping), but it's also pulled towards each of the three magnets. The combination of these forces creates a complex and chaotic system.

Basins of Attraction

The pendulum will eventually come to rest over one of the three magnets. The "basin of attraction" for a magnet is the set of all starting points that will lead the pendulum to that specific magnet. In the simulation, we color each starting point on the grid according to its final destination.

Fractal Boundaries

The result is not a simple map with clean borders. Instead, the boundaries between the colored regions are fractals. This means they have infinite detail and complexity. If you were to zoom in on a boundary between the red and blue regions, you would find that it's not a simple line, but is interwoven with points from the green region. No matter how deep you zoom, this complexity remains.

This is a profound visual demonstration of sensitive dependence on initial conditions, a hallmark of chaos. It means that in the boundary regions, a minuscule change in the starting position can lead to a completely different final outcome, making long-term prediction impossible.
