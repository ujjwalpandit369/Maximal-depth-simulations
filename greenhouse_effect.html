The Greenhouse Effect

A simplified energy balance model of a planet.

Overview

A planet's surface temperature is determined by a balance between the energy it receives from its star and the energy it radiates back into space. The Greenhouse Effect describes how a planet's atmosphere can disrupt this balance. Certain gases in the atmosphere are transparent to the incoming visible light from the sun, but opaque to the outgoing infrared (thermal) radiation from the surface. They absorb this outgoing heat and re-radiate some of it back down, warming the planet beyond the temperature it would have without an atmosphere. This simulation visualizes this energy balance and lets you explore how changing key parameters affects a planet's equilibrium temperature.
