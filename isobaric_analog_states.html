Isobaric Analog States: Nuclear Mirror Symmetry

Visualizing how the strong nuclear force creates nearly identical energy structures in different nuclei.

Estimated Runtime: Real-time | Recommended Device: Desktop | Approx. Size: ~150 KB

Overview

This simulation explores a fascinating concept in nuclear physics called "isobaric analog states." You will see the energy level diagrams of two special "mirror nuclei"—Carbon-13 and Nitrogen-13. Although they are different elements, the powerful strong nuclear force, which treats protons and neutrons almost identically, gives them a nearly identical set of energy states. The small difference you can see is a direct visual measurement of the effect of electric repulsion (the Coulomb force) inside a nucleus.
