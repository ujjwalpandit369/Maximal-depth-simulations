Spacetime Curvature: The Rubber Sheet Analogy

An interactive 3D visualization of Einstein's theory of gravity.

Plain-Language Overview

General Relativity reimagined gravity not as a force, but as a feature of spacetime itself. Massive objects warp the fabric of spacetime, and other objects simply follow the straightest possible path through this curved spacetime. This simulation provides the classic "rubber sheet" analogy. You can place massive objects onto the sheet to create "gravity wells," and then launch marbles to see their paths deflected by the curvature.

Instructions: Click on the grid to add a mass. Press "Roll Marble," then click and drag on the grid to set a marble's initial velocity.

Beginner's Guide

1. Flat Spacetime

Initially, the grid is flat. This represents spacetime with no mass. A marble rolled here would travel in a straight line.

2. Adding Mass, Warping Spacetime

Click on the grid to add a massive object. The grid deforms into a "gravity well." This is the first part of Einstein's idea: mass tells spacetime how to curve.

3. Motion in Curved Spacetime

Press "Roll Marble," then click and drag on the canvas to launch a test particle. Its trajectory is bent as it passes the massive object. This is the second part of the idea: curved spacetime tells matter how to move. The marble isn't being "pulled"; it's following the straightest possible path (a geodesic) on the curved surface.

Limitations of the Analogy

The rubber sheet analogy is powerful but has important limitations:

  1. It Uses Gravity to Explain Gravity: The ball sinks because of an external "downward" gravity. In reality, there is no extra dimension spacetime sinks into.
  2. It Only Shows Spatial Curvature: The sheet is 2D. General Relativity describes the curvature of 4D spacetime, including time. The warping of time is a crucial, missing component.
  3. Orbits are Not "Rolling Downhill": A marble in the simulation loses energy to friction. A planet in a stable orbit is in perpetual free-fall along its geodesic and does not lose energy this way.
