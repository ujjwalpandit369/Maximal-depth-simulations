Initial Population (x₀): A slider from 0 to 1.

Bifurcation Diagram: This is the main visual. It plots the long-term behavior (attractor points) of the system as a function of the parameter r.
Route to Chaos: Guide:
For low r, the system settles to a single fixed point.
As r increases, the system undergoes a "period-doubling" bifurcation: the attractor splits into 2 points, then 4, then 8...
This cascade accelerates until, at r ≈ 3.57, the system becomes chaotic, and the attractor points fill entire bands.
Within the chaos, there are "islands of stability" where the system becomes periodic again.
