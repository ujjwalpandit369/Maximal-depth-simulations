System of Blocks with Friction

An interactive simulation of static and kinetic friction.

Overview

This simulation explores the dynamics of two stacked blocks where a force is applied to the bottom block. The interaction is governed by the friction between the two blocks. If the applied force is small, the force of static friction is strong enough to "glue" the blocks together, and they accelerate as a single unit. However, there is a limit to how much force static friction can provide. If the applied force is large enough to require an acceleration greater than this limit, the top block will slip. Its motion will then be governed by the weaker kinetic friction as it slides across the surface of the bottom block.
