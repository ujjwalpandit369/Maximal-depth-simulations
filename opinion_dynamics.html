Sociophysics: Opinion Dynamics

An agent-based model of consensus formation and polarization.

Overview

How do large groups of people come to a consensus, and why do they sometimes fragment into polarized, echo-chamber-like factions? Sociophysics is a field that uses models from statistical physics to understand social phenomena. This simulation uses a bounded confidence model of opinion dynamics. It features a network of agents, each with an opinion on a spectrum. The key rule is that agents will only interact with and be influenced by neighbors whose opinions are already "close" to their own. This simple mechanism can lead to complex emergent behavior, including the formation of a global consensus or the persistent fragmentation of society into polarized groups.
